VegSense

Empowering Farmers with AI-Driven Organic Verification

➜ VegSense helps farmers verify crop authenticity, monitor soil and growth data, and connect their verified organic produce directly to trusted markets using AI, IoT, and data analytics.

Food markets today suffer from a serious trust gap:

✕

Consumer Trust Issue

Consumers can't visually identify if vegetables are truly organic.

✕

Farmer Authentication

Farmers struggle to prove the authenticity of their produce.

✕

Supply Chain Transparency

Retailers lack transparent systems to trace product origins.

✕

Health & Safety Concerns

Rising pesticide use creates serious health and safety concerns.

There is no simple, data-driven system that connects consumers, farmers, and retailers in one transparent ecosystem.

Software Platform Overview

➜

VegSense AI (AI Vision App)

Uses your smartphone camera to identify if a vegetable is organic or non-organic based on visual texture, color, and trained AI models.

  • Predicts "Organic" or "Non-Organic"
  • Shows nutrition and pesticide risk info
  • Cloud-based learning for accuracy improvement
➜

Smart Agriculture Dashboard

For farmers and retailers to register produce, upload data, and get instant organic certification scores.

  • Crop tracking, soil data, and pesticide record management
  • Digital organic certificates & QR code generation
  • Real-time analytics and reporting
➜

Community App

Consumers scan, review, and share organic produce data

  • Builds a verified database of trusted organic sellers
  • Encourages awareness through gamified education
  • Adds transparency and accountability

Hardware (IoT) Integration

➜

Smart Box (Hardware Device)

A compact IoT device that uses:

  • Spectroscopy Sensors – analyze chemical composition
  • Environmental Sensors – track humidity, soil data, and growth conditions
  • AI Processing Unit – interprets data using organic parameters
